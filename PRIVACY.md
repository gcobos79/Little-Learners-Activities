# Privacy Policy — Little Learners Activities

_Last updated: October 25, 2026. The Spanish version inside the app (ℹ️ → Privacidad) says the same._

**In short:** no ads, no tracking, no data sold. If a teacher uses an account, their activities
are stored in the cloud (Supabase) so they can be used on their tablets; otherwise everything
stays in the browser on that device.

## What is stored
- **Teacher (only with an account):** email address and a password managed by Supabase.
- First names (or nicknames) and an animal icon for each student the teacher adds.
- Quizzes and folders the teacher creates: text, pictures and audio.
- Quiz results: one per student, per quiz, per month (score, total, date, minutes, and the answer to each question). Repeating a quiz in the same month keeps only the latest attempt.
- Sonidos iniciales settings and that day's results (name and number correct; these results stay on the device only).
- A teacher PIN, stored as a one-way hash (the number itself is not stored).
- Natural-voice audio files generated for questions and answers.

## Where it is stored
- **On the device**, in the browser's local storage, which the app needs to work, including offline.
- **In the cloud, only with an account**, in Supabase (servers in the United States). Each teacher
  can only read and change their own data; the database enforces this for everyone else.

## What we don't do
- Children do not have accounts; they only tap their animal.
- No tracking cookies, ads or analytics.
- No location, camera or microphone access.
- No selling or sharing of information for advertising.

## Outside services
- **Supabase** (supabase.com): teacher sign-in and the cloud copy of the teacher's activities.
- **ElevenLabs** (elevenlabs.io): if the teacher turns on natural voices, the text of questions and
  answers is sent to ElevenLabs to create audio. Student names are not sent.
- **GitHub Pages** (when the site is opened from the internet): receives the visit like any website (e.g. IP address).
- The **device voice** (used when there is no natural voice) is provided by the browser; some online
  voices may send the text being read to the browser's provider.

## Children and schools
The app does not ask children for any information or create accounts for them. To protect their
privacy (COPPA and FERPA in the U.S.):
- Use first names or nicknames only.
- Don't put personal information about children in quiz questions.
- Backup files include students' first names: keep them somewhere safe and follow your school's rules.
- If your school requires a privacy agreement with cloud services, check before using an account.

## Deleting data
- **This device:** Maestro → Ajustes → “Borrar este dispositivo”.
- **The cloud:** Maestro → Ajustes → “Borrar mis datos de la nube” (voice files are deleted too).
- To close an account completely, ask the site administrator.

## Changes and contact
If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions: ask the teacher or school using
this site, or Geovanny Cobos.

_This is a clear, honest starting point, not legal advice. If a school or district adopts the site
officially, it should review this policy._
